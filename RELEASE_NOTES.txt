Milestones M1 and M2 — read-only. Nothing is ever written to the card.

Download the DelugeSampleManager-Setup .exe below and run it. It installs for your user only (no admin prompt).
Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: choose "More info" then "Run anyway".

What it does
- Finds the Deluge card in the reader (or choose any folder, such as a copy of the card).
- Browse the card and the sample library side by side; waveform, format and loop points for every file.
- Space plays and stops; "Play on select" auditions as you arrow through a folder.
- Shows which kits, synths and songs use each sample; double-click one to see the exact line.
- Health check: missing samples with likely matches, files the Deluge won't play,
  damaged preset files, unused samples, folder-layout issues, leftover clip takes.

If something goes wrong, use the Log folder button and send the dsm.log file.
