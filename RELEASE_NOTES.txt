Factory lock and Initialize Factory Samples. Version 0.6.0.

Download the DelugeSampleManager-Setup .exe below and run it; it updates the version you have.
Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: choose "More info" then "Run anyway".

New
- Initialize Factory Samples (toolbar) replaces "Factory move". Run it once, first: it moves the factory
  samples under SAMPLES/FACTORY (updating every kit, synth and song), makes the factory record, and copies
  every factory sample untouched into the library's FACTORY folder with a checksum. You see the folder
  list and every change before anything happens. Over USB the copy waits for the card reader.
- When a card that hasn't been initialized is opened, the app offers to initialize it.
- Factory samples are locked: the app won't rename, move, trash or write over them, nothing can be
  imported into SAMPLES/FACTORY (Import now offers Free and Paid only), and the library copies are
  read-only. Right-click shows "🔒 locked" instead of the actions.
- New Origin column on every sample: 🔒 Factory, Free, Paid, or Deluge (its own folders).
- Samples that are on the Deluge's card show their name in bold green, with a ✓ in the new On card
  column — in the library too, so you can see which originals are already on the card.

If something goes wrong, use the Log folder button and send the dsm.log file.
