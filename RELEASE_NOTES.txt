Factory record. Version 0.5.0.

Download the DelugeSampleManager-Setup .exe below and run it; it updates the version you have.
Windows may warn that the publisher is unknown: choose "More info" then "Run anyway".

New
- The Factory move now makes a factory record: every factory sample's original path, its new path under
  SAMPLES/FACTORY, its size and date. It is saved in the library (_DSM/factory-record.json, synced
  with Drive) and on the card (DSM/FACTORY_RECORD.JSON, outside SAMPLES so the Deluge never shows it).
- Straight after the move (with the card in the reader) the app offers to copy every factory sample,
  untouched, into the library's FACTORY folder, with a checksum for each in the record. Over USB the
  record is made at once and the copy waits for the card reader (it would take far too long over USB).
- Factory record (toolbar): what the record holds, how many are copied, anything missing or changed,
  and a button to copy whatever isn't copied yet. Running it again skips what's already done.
- The info panel shows "Factory sample — originally SAMPLES/…" and where its untouched copy is.
- Health check: a Factory samples section lists any factory sample missing from the card or no longer
  the size the factory made it.
- Undoing the factory move marks the record as undone.

If something goes wrong, use the Log folder button and send the dsm.log file.
